## 📢 Social Media & Community Feed Announcement (English)

**🏪 Own a sari-sari store, eatery, repair shop, or local business in Barangay Daine? Claim your listing on BrgyConnect today!**

To support our local MSMEs and micro-entrepreneurs, Barangay Daine I and II have curated community directory listings. If your establishment is already in the directory, you can now officially **claim ownership** in 3 simple steps:

1. 🔍 **Find your store** on the [BrgyConnect Business Directory](https://brgyconnect.gov.ph/directory).
2. 🏷️ Click the **"Claim this Business"** button on your listing.
3. 📝 Enter your contact number, upload a quick permit or ID photo, and submit.

Once verified by our Barangay staff, you will gain full access to:
- ⏰ Update your daily operating hours (with live Open/Closed badges!)
- 📸 Upload fresh storefront photos, menus, and price lists
- 💬 Connect your Facebook Messenger link for 1-click resident orders
- 💳 Highlight accepted payments like GCash & Maya

Together, let's strengthen local commerce and keep our community connected! 🇵🇭✨

## 🇵🇭 Pabatid sa Komunidad (Tagalog / Taglish)

**🏪 May-ari ka ba ng tindahan, karinderya, talyer, o negosyo dito sa Barangay Daine? I-claim na ang inyong tindahan sa BrgyConnect!**

Para sa lahat ng ating masisipag na negosyante sa Daine 1 at Daine 2: Nakatala na ba ang inyong tindahan sa aming Business Directory ngunit wala pa sa inyong personal account?

Maaari niyo na itong i-claim nang mabilis at libre:
1. Hanapin ang inyong pwesto sa **Business Directory**.
2. Pindutin ang **"Claim this Business"**.
3. Ibigay ang inyong contact info at mag-upload ng patunay (Barangay Clearance, Business Permit, o Valid ID).

Pagkatapos ma-verify ng Barangay Admin, kayo na mismo ang makakapag-update ng inyong:
- Live Opening & Closing Hours
- Menu at Price List
- GCash / Cash payment options
- Direct Messenger button para mabilis makontak ng mga kapitbahay!
